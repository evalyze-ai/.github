# Evalyze

Free tools for founders raising capital, from the team behind [Evalyze](https://www.evalyze.ai/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=github-profile), the AI fundraising platform for pre-seed to Series A founders.

Most founders lose weeks researching investors, writing emails that get deleted, and second-guessing every answer on an application. We build tools that fix the parts of fundraising that should be solvable, so founders can spend their time on the parts that aren't.

## What we ship

### [founder-fundraising-outreach](https://github.com/evalyze-ai/founder-fundraising-outreach)
A Claude skill that helps founders write investor outreach that gets replies and reads like a human wrote it. It covers cold emails, follow-ups, replies to investor questions, due diligence prep, post-meeting recaps and pipeline stall recovery, all calibrated to how real fundraises run.

**v0.10, in pilot with founders.** [Install it](https://github.com/evalyze-ai/founder-fundraising-outreach#install)

### [awesome-startup-fundraising](https://github.com/evalyze-ai/awesome-startup-fundraising)
A curated list of tools for evaluating startups, improving pitch materials, finding investors and running a raise from start to finish.

### [yc-application-coach](https://github.com/evalyze-ai/yc-application-coach)
A Claude skill that helps founders write a Y Combinator application a partner can understand in one read, check their founder video, and prep for the 10-minute interview. It drafts from facts you can back up, in your own voice, pressure-tests the idea before you write, and runs mock interviews, and it never invents a number or quotes acceptance odds. It's built on 72 published YC applications, about 210 founder write-ups and posts, and YC's own pages, checked in October 2026.

**v1.0, October 2026.** [Install it](https://github.com/evalyze-ai/yc-application-coach#install)

## What we're building

[Evalyze](https://www.evalyze.ai/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=github-profile) is an AI fundraising platform for pre-seed to Series A founders, with 15K+ founders and 20K+ verified investors on it. It finds the investors most likely to back you, reviews your deck, and runs outreach you approve before anything sends, all in one place.

- [Investor Matching](https://www.evalyze.ai/investor-matching?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=github-profile) ranks investors by stage, sector, location, check size and investment history.
- [Investor Outreach](https://www.evalyze.ai/outreach?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=github-profile) drafts personalized emails that send from your own inbox once you approve them, and tracks replies and follow-ups.
- [Startup Analyzer](https://www.evalyze.ai/startup-analyzer?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=github-profile) scores your startup against companies at your stage, including an investor readiness score.
- [Pitch Deck Coach](https://www.evalyze.ai/pitch-coach?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=github-profile) scores your deck out of 100 with slide-by-slide feedback.
- [Managed Fundraising](https://www.evalyze.ai/managed-fundraising?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=github-profile) puts an ex-VC-led team on your raise for one flat monthly fee, with no equity or success fee.

The Starter plan is free, with no card required. [See plans and pricing](https://www.evalyze.ai/pricing?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=github-profile)
